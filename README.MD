## Revenue Rescue
## E-Commerce Revenue, Customer & Operations Intelligence
Revenue Rescue is an end-to-end data analytics project designed
to identify revenue opportunities, customer retention patterns,
delivery problems and potential revenue leakage.
## Business Questions
- How much revenue is generated?
- Which product categories generate the most revenue?
- Which customers return?
- What is the repeat customer rate?
- How often are orders delivered late?
- Are late deliveries associated with lower review scores?
- Where are potential revenue leakage points? 
## Tech Stack
- Python
- Pandas
- SQL
- DuckDB
- Plotly
- Streamlit
- Git/GitHub
## Key Analysis
### Revenue
Analyzed revenue by product category and seller.
### Customers
Measured one-time vs repeat customers.
### Operations
Calculated delivery duration and late delivery rate.
### Customer Experience
Compared review scores between late and on-time deliveries.
## Business Recommendations
The analysis identifies areas where an e-commerce
business can investigate delivery performance,
customer retention and revenue leakage.
## Dataset
Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.
The raw dataset is not included in this repository.
